# About Everyday

Everyday v1.2.33 is an artistic, native Android productivity app by Sukrit Ghosh.
Android 11 or newer. It combines Today, Tasks, Habits, Calendar, Expenses and
Insights, Folio block notebooks, Aspirations, day modes and optional Cycle tracking.
Custom fonts, light/dark gradients, reduced effects and manual scoped backups
are included. App records stay on the device; the app has no INTERNET permission.
