About Everyday

An artistic native Android app by Sukrit Ghosh, designed for everyday plans, spending and creative notes.

Version 1.2.33 · Android 11/API 30 minimum.

Today, Tasks, Habits, Calendar, Expenses and Insights, Folio, Aspirations, day modes and optional Cycle tracking share one local workspace. The app has no INTERNET permission, advertising or analytics, and backups are initiated by you.

Folio follows a structured notebook approach: vertical editable blocks, nested pages, images, tables, lists and movable content. See the guide for what is available now.

This download is a debug-signed prototype for testing. The app source is proprietary and held in a separate private repository; this private downloads repository contains download information and help.
